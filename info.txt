Destruction: Harnesses fire, frost, and shock elements to inflict direct damage

Restoration: Restores vital health and magicka while warding off attacks and undead

Conjuration: Summons Daedra, bound weapons, and reanimated undead to fight for you

Illusion: Alters perceptions to calm, terrify, confuse, or deceive enemy minds

Alteration: Manipulates physical laws to provide armor, utility, and crowd control
